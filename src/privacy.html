Local application data

Rundowns, language, appearance and recovery data are stored in this browser’s localStorage. sessionStorage holds tab-specific context and the selected view. A service worker stores application files in Cache Storage for offline use. Timer recovery does not preserve unsaved editor drafts.

Purposes and retention

Local storage enables reusable sequences, preferences, timer recovery and offline operation. Rundowns and recovery records may remain locally; no fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Browser policies and clearing site data affect retention. Exported files are under your control.

JSON files and sharing

You initiate exports and imports. The application does not automatically upload JSON backups to a server. Exports contain rundown content; check titles for confidential information before sharing. Active timers are not included.

Website access and hosting

The website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich, Germany. Technical connection data, including IP addresses, are processed to deliver the website and help keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in reliable and secure operation). The hosting setting for regular access-log files is disabled; this does not rule out necessary connection processing or security-related processing by the host. See the hosting provider’s privacy information for its own processing.

External services and email

The application uses local assets and system fonts. It does not integrate Google Account, Google Drive or Google Analytics. If you contact the operator by email, your email address, message and associated communication data are processed to handle and respond to your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in communication). The contact mailbox is provided through Google Gmail. Google may process the communication data, including outside the European Economic Area, under its applicable safeguards. See Google’s privacy policy.

External links and analytics

External links open only when selected and are subject to their destinations’ own privacy notices. easyTimer does not currently deploy Google Analytics or other application-level visitor analytics. Any future analytics integration would require an updated privacy notice and, where necessary, prior consent.

Retention of contact messages

Contact correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and for any applicable legal obligations.

Browser storage

Browser storage is used for essential application functionality, user-selected preferences, timer recovery and offline use. These data remain in your browser unless you export them yourself. You can remove them through your browser’s site-data controls; doing so can delete your saved rundowns and recovery state.

Your rights and supervision

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. Contact the controller using the email address above. You may lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

Rundowns, Sprache, Darstellung und Wiederherstellungsdaten werden im localStorage dieses Browsers gespeichert. sessionStorage enthält tabbezogenen Kontext und die gewählte Ansicht. Zwecke und Speicherdauer

Lokale Speicherung ermöglicht wiederverwendbare Abläufe, Einstellungen, Timer-Wiederherstellung und Offline-Betrieb. Rundowns und Wiederherstellungsdaten können lokal erhalten bleiben; eine feste automatische Löschfrist wird nicht zugesagt. Browserregeln und Löschen von Website-Daten beeinflussen die Aufbewahrung. JSON-Dateien und Weitergabe

Export und Import werden von dir ausgelöst. Die Anwendung lädt JSON-Sicherungen nicht automatisch auf einen Server hoch. Exporte enthalten Rundown-Inhalte; prüfe Titel vor einer Weitergabe auf vertrauliche Informationen. Aktive Timer sind nicht enthalten.
