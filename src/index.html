What is easyTimer?

easyTimer is a free online timer for meetings, workshops, retrospectives and focused work. Start a single countdown or organize several activities into a timed event.

Prepare events with phases, durations, information and links. Save reusable Event Templates to create independent events.

easyTimer works directly in your browser without installation or a required account.
